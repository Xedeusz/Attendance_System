# Attendance corrections — Phase 5
Employee submits at /employee/attendance/corrections → another eligible HR user decides at /hr/attendance/corrections → Employee receives a persistent decision notice. Supervisors are denied every correction route and attendance/photo route, including their own.

Types: forgotten/incorrect Time In or Time Out, missing record, camera/GPS problem and other. The employee supplies local proposed datetime values, requested date and a reason. Existing-record IDs are constrained to the authenticated employee. Corrections for pending evidence must wait for the original HR evidence review.

Pending duplication is blocked by a unique employee/record-or-date key. Future times, inverted intervals and inconsistent dates are rejected. Review additionally checks other accepted/effective sessions, approved supplemental entries and record revision. Stale requests return 409, including genuine camera punches recorded after a request was submitted. Original-source snapshots and correction revisions are rechecked under the transaction lock. Required HR remarks and confirmation apply to approval and rejection; HR cannot review their own correction.

Original record timestamps, server event facts, GPS results, photos and verification status remain unchanged. Approval records effective_time_in_at/effective_time_out_at and increments correction_version. Attendance details compare the original evidence and effective manual values; history shows a manual correction label. Original captured session_status remains historical; effective checkout excludes the record from open-session queries and the generated unique open-employee guard, permitting a later genuine Time In.

A completely missing or rejected-record manual session requires a complete Time In/Time Out interval; this avoids an open manual session without a supported camera checkout. A completely missing record remains a supplemental approved manual correction entry, visible in personal attendance history and HR correction monitoring. It does not manufacture an AttendanceRecord, AttendanceEvent, photograph, reported location or location-validated flag. Reject/cancel never applies an effective change. Original_values snapshots and reviewer/time/remarks preserve correction history.

This phase does not add correction attachments, schedule-based lateness/absence, payroll adjustments, approved manual-entry reversal, attendance reclassification or falsified camera/GPS verification. Independent review criteria, deadlines, supporting evidence, retention and reversal policies need company confirmation. Existing assistance requests remain a separate support queue and do not automatically create corrections.
